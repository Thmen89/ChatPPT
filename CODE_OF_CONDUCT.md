# Code of Conduct

ChatPPT is a small open-source project. Everyone who takes part in its issues, pull requests
and discussions is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and help newcomers find their way.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussion.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
